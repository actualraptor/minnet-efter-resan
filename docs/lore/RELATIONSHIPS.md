Relationship state is stored per character in `GameState.relationships[char_id] = {affinity, trust, notes[]}`. It is separate from reputation. Values below are the milestone-001 starting points.

| Freyja ↔ Ragna, Edvin | former party | fond, distant | [PROPOSAL] |

## Rules [CANON]
- Love is shown through remembered preferences, time given and small promises, more than confession.
- Not every relationship needs tragedy. Edda's story is warm and complete without her dying.
- Callbacks must reference events the player actually experienced (memory events).
