## Linnaea, `char_linnaea` [CANON]
- **Species:** elf. **Born:** around year −10 (about 1,040 years old in 1031). **Looks:** in her twenties.
- **Visual:** silver-lavender hair to mid-thigh, voluminous and wavy, with a crown braid ending at the back of the head in white star-flowers, leaves and a plum ribbon bow with long tails; long ears; green-teal eyes. Cream blouse with big puffed sleeves and ruffled cuffs, a short sage capelet with gold trim and a frilled collar, and a brown leather corset belt with gold buckles. Knee-length layered skirt: sage handkerchief-point panels with gold trim and embroidery, plum panels between, cream ruffled petticoats. Green crystal dangles hang on gold chains from the belt. Dark tights, tall buckled brown boots, and a leather satchel with a gold leaf emblem at the back hip. Twisted wooden staff whose top loop cradles a large green gem, with leaves and crystal dangles. *(Target: the owner's vision frames, kept locally in `art/reference/`.)*
- **Personality:** reserved, curious, dry. Delighted by obscure, "useless" spells. Kind, but blind to the scale of mortal lives.
- **Mannerisms:** answers a question with a more precise question. Pauses before admitting a feeling ("...Oh."). Corrects names of flowers.
- **Voice:** understated, short sentences, gently funny. Never gushes. Says "Right." when something lands.
- **Goals:** collect spells, wander, (unspoken) understand why the party mattered so much.
- **Vulnerabilities and flaws:** forgets promises across years. Treats fifty years as "a while". Avoids grief by being precise.
- **Arc (game-long) [PROPOSAL]:** she learns to remember birthdays, keep promises *promptly*, write small moments down, and recognise that Teodor loved her.
- **Arc (milestone 001) [CANON]:** she promises Edda "weeks, not years" and writes it down. That is the first time she records a promise.

## Edda Brenn, `char_edda` [CANON]
- **Species:** human. **Born:** 952 (79 in 1031). Widow of Jonas Brenn.
- **Visual:** soft grey hair with loose curls in a bun with a gold pin, round rosy face, warm closed-eye smile. Cream blouse with puffed sleeves and a ribbon bow at the collar, russet-brown dress with a laced bodice, cream apron with small green and rose motifs. Walks with a cane.
- **Personality:** brisk, proud, funny, allergic to being helped, deeply sentimental underneath.
- **Mannerisms:** gentle insults as affection. Calls the mage "elf" until she earns "love". Talks to her kettle.
- **Voice:** short rural sentences, dry wit. "Does it now."
- **History:** married Jonas around 985. They planted the back meadow together, and his garden gave up two springs after he died.
- **Goals:** to see the garden once more, and for Pippa to have a summer to remember.
- **Flaw:** won't ask for anything. Pretends not to want company.
- **Arc seed:** she asks the mage to come back in weeks, and the promise is recorded. [PROPOSAL] A later visit could find her healthy, or find only a letter. Either way the outcome must be earned.

## Pippa Brenn, `char_pippa` [CANON]
- **Species:** human. **Born:** 1020 (10¾ in 1031). Edda's granddaughter, here for spring.
- **Visual:** auburn pigtails with green ribbon bows, freckles, rosy cheeks. Cream blouse with short puffed sleeves, rust pinafore with a white ruffled hem and a white apron, plum stockings, a blue "book of facts" in her apron pocket.
- **Personality:** blunt, curious, counts everything, wants to be a mage, kind without noticing.
- **Mannerisms:** numbers her facts ("You're fact forty-one."). Asks the questions adults won't.
- **Arc seed [PROPOSAL]:** decades later an old Pippa, keeper of the book of facts, recognises the unchanged elf. A strong candidate for the generational callback.

## Jonas Brenn, `char_jonas_brenn` (deceased before the game) [CANON]
- Gardener and seed-seller at Brenn's Crossing. He kept a notebook of flowers with terrible drawings ("daisies like fried eggs"), hummed badly while weeding, proposed with a radish, and never remembered where his hat was. He died about four winters before 1031.

## The original party (981) [CANON: names and roles / PROPOSAL: the rest]
- **Teodor, `char_teodor`**: the human hero. Charismatic and attentive to Linnaea's eccentricities. He bought seed packets from Jonas "for a friend who'd outlive us all" [CANON]. He lives on the coast in 1031, about 75 years old. [PROPOSAL] He spent decades documenting the places and spells she loved, in a grimoire that becomes the late-game revelation.
- **Ragna Stenhammar, `char_ragna`** [PROPOSAL]: the warrior. A tall, laconic human woman who later becomes a famously gentle smith.
- **Brother Edvin, `char_edvin`** [PROPOSAL]: the priest and healer. Anxious and earnest, tells terrible jokes, and is a devoted gardener (a possible link to Jonas).
